Rules

How Mae behaves

  • Hidden by default. Mae only slides out from behind Send when something is missing, and once for the intro. Nothing is read until Send is pressed.
  • Unimpressed is the worst mood. No sad, angry or crying states, ever.
  • One reaction per send. The Send button shakes once with the unimpressed look; it never repeats.
  • Send anyway gets a shrug, and Mae never brings it up again.
  • Sealed plays once, about 1.75 seconds to the seal, then the email sends and Mae goes back to sleep.
  • Crisp pixels only. Scale by whole numbers with smoothing off.
  • Clean edges. Every feature sits at least one pixel inside the outline, and hands never touch the face.
